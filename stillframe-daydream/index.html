Stillframe Daydream

Your photos, on the TV, even when you're not watching.

A remote-friendly photo gallery and system screensaver for Android TV / Google TV. It shows your Google Photos in five display modes, with configurable transitions, daily active hours, on-device framing, and a persistent photo cache.

Five layouts

  • Fullscreen — one photo at a time, with Ken Burns pan/zoom or a photo-color textured mat with a recessed bevel and smooth fades.
  • Mosaic — a tiled grid, per-photo or per-tile cadence.
  • Gallery collage — arranged photos, with fades, slides/wipes, flips, and shrink transitions.
  • Photo Pile — overlapping photos added one at a time, on black or textured linen.
  • Side by side — two photos at once.

Playback controls work in the system screensaver too: Left/Right visits previous/next photos, OK remembers the shown photo's type, Back exits.

Screensaver, on your schedule

  • Screensaver preferences — a separate fixed layout or automatic rotation through selected favorites, with interval and shuffle controls.
  • System screensaver selection — set Stillframe as the TV's system screensaver from TV settings, with a restore-previous option to undo it.
  • Daily active hours — local-time start/stop windows, including overnight. Outside scheduled hours, the scene goes black and releases the screen-on request.

Offline cache and smart framing

  • Offline cache — configurable 64 MB–8 GB (default 512 MB), with deduplication, storage limits, source-aware cleanup, and network/quota fallback, so cached photos keep playing without a connection.
  • Smart framing and 360° — local face/visual-interest cropping, conservative panorama detection, perspective projection, and optional slow pan.
  • Google diagnostics — failed operation, HTTP/Google error, failure time, retry delay, and last successful request, preserved during cached playback.

About Google Photos access

Google Photos uses the Ambient API, not the Picker. A working integration requires an eligible Google app registration; creating a generic Cloud project does not automatically provide Ambient access, and the Google Photos partner program requires acceptance before a project can use Ambient. Sign-in can succeed while photo listing is rejected, and the app's Photo loading status shows the exact error and cooldown.

New sign-ins request the photosambient.mediaitems scope to display selected photos, plus openid email to identify the connected account in Settings. No profile scope is requested, and email is used only for that identification — see the Privacy Policy.
